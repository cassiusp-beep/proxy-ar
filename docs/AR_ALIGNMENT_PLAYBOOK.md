# AR alignment playbook

The working reference for debugging how PROXY locks the cartoon city onto the physical models. Read it before touching alignment code, calibration constants, or the fidelity scan. Update the **Measurements log** after every device test.

The goal is a city that stays on the static tangible models (the diorama/trophy print now, the Field Unit pins later) to about 1–2 mm from any viewpoint, with a repeatable check that proves it before a demo.

## 1. Operating rules (agency and limits)

**I ask first before:**

- Changing physical instructions: marker size, print, trimming, placement.
- Editing anything outside `AR Experience/`, such as `../Diorama/diorama.py`.
- Changing the brief's design (setups, sync-dock story, what the city looks like).
- Anything a launch would interrupt while the user is mid-test (wait for "done").

**Acceptance criteria for saving a calibration:**
1. Fit RMS after ≤ 4.5 mm and stable across scans, with ≥ 2,000 matched points and ≥ 70% coverage. (Revised from 3 mm: every scan so far settles at about 4 mm, which is the LiDAR's noise floor on this model.)
2. Correction is small: |shift| ≤ 5 mm, |yaw| ≤ 1.5°. A bigger correction means something physical is wrong (placement, print scale). Find it instead of absorbing it.
3. Repeatable: a second scan after re-locking agrees within ±1 mm and ±0.3°.
4. Visual: a user photo after Apply shows no gray seams along the edges or behind the buildings.

**Suspended (2026-10-27):** no fit may be applied or saved until the fit models the LiDAR bias along each ray (§5 H1). Applied fits made the overlay worse in session 3.

Never save a fit that moves the city vertically. Height comes from the markers themselves; the LiDAR table plane is only logged (DECISIONS 012, 017).

## 2. How alignment works now

1. **Marker detection and averaging:** 50 mm images, tracked while sampling (`maximumNumberOfTrackedImages = 2`), off after the lock. `MarkerSampler` keeps the latest 30 detections per marker: circular-mean heading with outliers dropped, position = median of the inliers (samples within 5 mm of the median).
2. **Duplicate guard** (DECISIONS 018, `MarkerLock.samplesConsistent`): a marker whose samples have < 80% inliers or heading spread > 0.5° is refused. No lock, one `lock refused:` warning, samplers restart, pill "Marker seen twice? Hide extra copies" (clears ~1 s after no marker is seen).
3. **Pose** (`MarkerLock.swift`). **Diorama:** two markers (`CalibrationMarker` left, `CalibrationMarkerRight`), heading from the 96.35 mm line between their centers, anchor at the left marker's center placed on that line at 48.2 mm from the midpoint (`lockPair`, `pairPose`). Locks when both have ≥ 15 samples; left-only after 3 s without the right (5 s if the right was seen briefly). A pair off by > 20° or > 15 mm falls back to the left marker. Logs one `PAIR key=value` line. **Field Unit:** one marker (`lockSingle`).
4. **Leveled lock** (`leveledPose`, `MarkerLock.swift`): keep position and heading; up comes from gravity (world Y). **Height comes from the markers** (pair: mean of L.y and R.y; single: that marker's y), with no offset (DECISIONS 017). The LiDAR table plane is raycast (`.existingPlaneGeometry`, then `.estimatedPlane`) and **only logged**: `Height: marker …, plane …, diff … (not applied)`. diff = plane + 0.1 mm paper − marker, so **+ means the plane sits above the markers**. A warning fires above 3 mm; planes more than 15 mm off are skipped as another surface. The 15 mm gate is wider than the marble base (pin origin 7.6 mm above the table), so a plane on the base top would still be logged, with a warning.
   - **Sync resets world tracking**, so world poses can't be compared across Sync locks. **Re-lock (keep world)** keeps the frame and logs a `RELOCK` line against the previous lock.
5. **Offset:** `offsetEntity.transform` = setup constant (Field Unit or Diorama), plus yaw and scale. Content frame: meters from the pin origin (`PINS_ORIGIN`, Rhino (5, 0, 7.58) mm), with `(x, y, z) = (X, Z, −Y)/1000` in Rhino terms. The front of the models, where the player stands, is Rhino +X, so the yaw relative to the marker is −90°.
6. **World anchor:** a plain `ARAnchor` at the leveled pose, so content stays when the marker leaves view. World tracking uses LiDAR scene reconstruction.
7. **Fidelity scan** (`DepthScan.swift`, `FidelityFit.swift`): high-confidence `sceneDepth` points on the model's footprint (4 mm inset, within 20 mm of the terrain), with a 16 × 16 coverage grid. It auto-fits at 75% coverage. Gauss-Newton with Huber weights over x, z, yaw, plus a vertical nuisance term (LiDAR bias, reported and never applied). Points are scored against the 0.5 mm heightfield in `terrain_height.bin`.

**Diorama placement:** both papers trimmed to the black border, top edges against the marble base's front face (Rhino X 182.8). Left marker's left edge in line with the base's left side (Rhino Y 0), center Rhino (207.8, 25.0, 0.1) mm. Right marker's right edge in line with the base's right side (Rhino Y 146.35), center (207.8, 121.35). This gives `DIORAMA_MARKER_TO_ORIGIN = (−0.0250, 0.0075, −0.2028)` (geometric; the 3.3 mm saved correction was reverted), yaw −90°. Expected physical check: terrain flush with the base's left and right sides, terrain front face 19.2 mm behind the base's front face (Rhino X 163.6 vs 182.8). Terrain front edge is ~4.5 mm tall above the pin origin.

**Field Unit placement (the critique setup, 2026-10-28):** the 50 mm guides in the sync dock, center (246.574, 34.6, 6.0) mm from the pin origin, giving `MARKER_TO_ORIGIN = (−0.0346, −0.0062, −0.2466)`, yaw −90° (y = −(6.0 floor + 0.2 sticker) mm). Not yet tested on device. One marker only, so the single-marker lock (`lockSingle`) and its heading error are what matter (§3a).
- **Dock geometry (Rhino, user screenshot 11):** pocket walls Rhino X 180.974–281.574, Y 4.6–105.2 (100.6 mm square), floor Z 13.58, so the pocket center is (226.274, 54.9, 6.0) mm from the pin origin. The 50 mm marker's front edge (Rhino +X, toward the player) is 5 mm from the pocket's front wall. Under a rigid 100 mm card it doesn't matter (debossed, below the card's support), but the card hides it.

Seen from the player's side at 45°, the front wall hides the front 4–8 mm of the 50 mm marker; at 60°, 0–2.4 mm; at 70°+, none. *Lock from above, looking down at ≥ 60°* (as when lifting the phone straight up out of the dock).

- **Option, a 100 mm dock marker (not built):** a rigid card cut to ~100.4 mm, registered by the pocket walls, resting on the 1 mm guides. Center = the pocket center, so `MARKER_TO_ORIGIN = (−0.0549, −(7.0 + t)/1000, −0.2263)`, yaw −90°, with t the card's printed-surface height above the guides (≈ −0.0072 for a 0.2 mm card). ## 8. Researched resources

- **[Barath19/Boxer3D](https://github.com/Barath19/Boxer3D)** (Swift, ARKit + LiDAR). It uses the same `sceneDepth` + intrinsics pipeline as `DepthScan` (identical intrinsics scaling), which confirms our unprojection math. Useful ideas: median depth per 16 × 16 patch for robustness; the gravity vector for orientation. Its core is ML 3D box lifting (YOLO11n + BoxerNet, ~400 MB ONNX), which is cm-level boxing of generic objects, not mm registration to a known model. Don't adopt the model.
- **[akharroubi/AerialLidarClassifier](https://github.com/akharroubi/AerialLidarClassifier)**: a QGIS plugin for semantic segmentation of airborne, mobile-mapping and rail LiDAR (LitePT point transformer, 5–10 cm voxels, NVIDIA GPU, GPL-3.0). Not applicable: wrong scale and platform. The scan already separates model points geometrically (footprint, plus within 20 mm of the terrain).
- **[humanscode.site/ios-ar](https://humanscode.site/ios-ar)**: an agency services page with no technical content. Its one data point is a vendor claim of "±5 mm" LiDAR precision (unverified), consistent with the per-point noise we assume.
